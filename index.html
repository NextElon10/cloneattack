How it works

  1. 1. Paste. Your own bio or recent posts: the same thing a stranger sees.
  2. 2. Attack. AI plays the scammer and writes the 3 most likely attacks, showing exactly which details it used.
  3. 3. Defend. Hide a detail and the attacks get vaguer while your Cloneability score drops. Then apply the fixes on your real profiles.

About: a fire drill, not a weapon

  • Built for checking your own public footprint.
  • Every attack is stamped SIMULATION. No links, numbers or UPI IDs are ever generated, and there's no send button.
  • Nothing is stored or logged. Your text is used once and discarded.
